WM Keyboard is an Android keyboard that keeps its intelligence on your phone. Prediction, autocorrect, glide typing, transliteration, emoji search and learning are all local computation over files in the app's own storage. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising, no account and no server of ours for the app to phone home to.

1,566 LANGUAGES, 3,398 LAYOUTS

Native scripts, InScript variants, romanized entries, CJK and constructed languages, all from one data-driven registry. A fresh install starts in the languages your phone is already set to and suggests more from your system language list.

BENGALI THE WAY YOU ACTUALLY TYPE IT

Avro-compatible phonetic typing, so "ami valo achi" becomes আমি ভালো আছি. The phonetic index is lenient: "asi", "achi" and "achhi" all reach the right word. Probhat is there too, with aspirates on shift, plus conjunct-aware backspace.

TYPING THAT LEARNS, LOCALLY

A trie-lattice beam decoder shared by tapping and glide, n-gram context ranking, autocorrect gated by a likelihood ratio rather than a blunt threshold, and on-device learning of your words, bigrams and trigrams. One tap clears all of it.

A REAL TOOLBOX

Clipboard with history, pinning and search. Snippets and text expansion. Calculator, unit and currency conversion, calendars, translate, dictionary lookup, web search, camera, OCR and QR scanning, media controls, an app launcher and more, most of them fully offline.

EMOJI, GIFS AND STICKERS

The Emoji 17.0 catalog with semantic search in 125 languages, per-person skin tones, kaomoji, custom sticker packs, and long-press to send animated emoji.

MAKE IT YOURS

A full theme editor: palettes, fonts, key shapes, textures, decals, particles and photo backgrounds, plus Material You and AMOLED. Resize the keyboard, split it, float it, or run it one-handed. Themes and layouts export and import as files, and an addon repository can serve you more.

VOICE, HANDWRITING AND AI

Speech recognition with a continuous mode, fully offline Whisper dictation, handwriting input, grammar checking, and optional AI writing tools where you bring your own API key. Nothing is enabled behind your back.

PRIVACY

Typing never uses the network. Online tools such as translate, search, GIFs and cloud AI send what you give them only when you use them, and each one is documented. Three things can reach the network on their own, and each has its own switch: a language you enable fetches its n-gram pack and emoji dictionary once, the currency chip fetches the day's rates the first time it sees an amount, and the weather chip fetches the forecast for a location you saved. Password and secure fields turn off learning, suggestions and clipboard capture on their own, and incognito mode does the same on demand. Backups are written where you point them, and a passphrase encrypts them.

Handwriting, OCR, scanning and the on-device models are built on Google's ML Kit, which reports its own diagnostics to Google while you use those features. The privacy policy says exactly what: https://wmkeyboard.pages.dev/privacy/policy/

The full documentation, including exactly what touches the network and when, is at https://wmkeyboard.pages.dev
