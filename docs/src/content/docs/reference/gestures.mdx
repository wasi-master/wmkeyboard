## Swiping the spacebar

<SettingsPath path="Typing / Gestures" />

Which action a spacebar swipe runs depends on *when* the drag starts relative to the press-and-hold delay (300&nbsp;ms by default), not on how far it travels. A drag that crosses the slop before the delay elapses uses the **Quick swipe on the spacebar** slot. One that starts after uses the **Press and hold, then swipe** slot. Full mechanics on [Gestures](/typing/gestures/) and [Switching languages](/languages/switching/).

| Gesture | Where | What it does | Setting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tap, no movement | Spacebar | Types a space | None |
| Swipe starting before the press-and-hold delay | Spacebar | Runs the quick-swipe slot's action | Quick swipe on the spacebar (default Language) |
| Hold past the press-and-hold delay, then swipe | Spacebar | Runs the hold-swipe slot's action | Press and hold, then swipe (default Cursor) |
| Slot = Language, swipe sideways | Spacebar | Steps through enabled layouts (a flick moves one; a continued swipe steps every 44&nbsp;dp after a 200&nbsp;ms pause, 110&nbsp;dp to wrap past either end) | Either slot set to Language |
| Slot = Cursor, swipe sideways | Spacebar | Moves the text cursor one character per 16&nbsp;dp of drag | Either slot set to Cursor |
| Same, dragging vertically | Spacebar | Also steps the cursor a line at a time | 2-D cursor touchpad (Off) |
| Swipe up at least 80&nbsp;dp, more up than sideways, then keep moving | Spacebar | Opens the 2-D touchpad for that drag: the cursor follows in every direction until you lift. The cursor holds still for the long-press delay after it opens, and up and down count once the finger pauses or turns, so the opening swipe never jumps a line | Swipe up on the spacebar set to Touchpad (default Nothing) |
| Swipe up at least 80&nbsp;dp, more up than sideways | Spacebar | Opens the number pad at once | Swipe up on the spacebar set to Number pad |
| Swipe up at least 80&nbsp;dp, more up than sideways, then lift | Spacebar | Runs the action picked for the swipe up, once, at the lift; types no space | Swipe up on the spacebar set to Another action |
| Same, while the drag moves the cursor | Spacebar | Shows the text at the cursor, enlarged, in a bubble over the text | Cursor magnifier (On) |
| Slot = Numpad, cross the initial slop | Spacebar | Opens the numeric keypad (one-shot, so the rest of the drag does nothing) | Either slot set to Numpad |
| Slot = Another action, cross the initial slop, then lift | Spacebar | Runs the action picked for that slot (a tool such as Text editing, an edit, a layer, a layout, another keyboard, text), once, at the lift; types no space | Either slot set to Another action |
| Straight downward drag, steeper than it is wide | Spacebar | Dismisses the keyboard | Swipe down to hide the keyboard (Off) |
| Swipe in from the left or right edge, at least 40&nbsp;dp, more across than up or down | Any key within 16&nbsp;dp of the side | Back: closes the open panel, or the keyboard | Swipe in from the edge to go back (Off) |
| Press and hold (~250&nbsp;ms, or your press-and-hold delay if shorter), with two or more layouts enabled | Spacebar | Opens the inline language preview or a scrollable picker list | None |
| Drag once the preview or picker is open | Spacebar | Always switches language, regardless of which slot is configured | None |

## Dragging a delete key to delete words

<SettingsPath path="Key press / Delete keys" />

Tap, hold-to-repeat, and the delete swipe are one state machine on <KeyCap>⌫</KeyCap>, so a drag can take over from the repeat loop mid-press. In Words mode each word costs less to delete than the last: 72&nbsp;dp for the first, 56&nbsp;dp for the second, then 6&nbsp;dp less per word down to a 28&nbsp;dp floor. In Characters mode every character costs a flat 20&nbsp;dp. Full breakdown, including the character-run and selection rules, on [Gestures](/typing/gestures/#deleting-more-than-a-character-with-backspace).

| Gesture | Where | What it does | Setting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tap | Backspace | Deletes one character | None |
| Hold still | Backspace | Repeats character deletion after the repeat wait, at the delete interval | Time before a key repeats · Delete repeat interval |
| Drag left past a small slop | Backspace | Cancels the repeat and takes a word (shrinking with each word) or a character per step | Swipe to delete · Swipe deletes (On · Words) |
| Drag right mid-swipe | Backspace | Gives back one step of the highlight per step of travel | Show what you will delete (On) |
| Lift after dragging left | Backspace | Deletes everything the swipe highlighted | (same) |
| Drag right mid-swipe | Backspace | Re-anchors instead of undeleting, so the next leftward word costs full travel again | Show what you will delete (Off) |
| Hold still | Either delete key | Repeats a whole word per tick instead of a character, at the word interval | Holding delete clears words · Word delete interval (Off · 140 ms) |
| Tap | Forward delete | Deletes the character after the cursor, with its marks and any emoji parts | None |
| Drag right past a small slop | Forward delete | Cancels the repeat and takes a word or a character after the cursor per step | Swipe on forward delete · Swipe deletes (On · Words) |
| Drag left mid-swipe | Forward delete | Gives back one step of the highlight per step of travel | Show what you will delete (On) |
| Lift after dragging right | Forward delete | Deletes everything the swipe highlighted | (same) |

## Flicking a key with arms

<SettingsPath path="Typing / Gestures" />

A key with flick directions (the Japanese flick pad, the English flick (3×3) board, any key given arms in the layout editor, or a key given [swipes on keys](/typing/gestures/#swipes-on-keys) in Settings) reads a short swipe off it as a flick. Full mechanics on [Flick keys](/typing/gestures/#flick-keys).

| Gesture | Where | What it does | Setting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tap | Flick key | Types the centre character | None |
| Tap | Swipe-only flick key (no centre) | Types nothing, or shows the key's flick popup for a moment | Tap a swipe-only key to see its swipes (Off) |
| Swipe past the flick distance in one of eight directions | Flick key | Types the arm at that edge or corner; an arm that runs an action runs it, and a cursor or delete arm repeats while held | Flick distance (22&nbsp;dp) |
| Swipe towards an edge or corner with no arm | Flick key | Types the centre character | None |
| Swipe out to a letter arm and more than halfway back | Flick key | Types that arm's capital (or its **with Shift** form) | None; an arm with no capital cancels to the centre instead |
| Hold still inside the flick distance | Flick key | Opens the press-and-hold alternates, as on any key | Press and hold hints (On) |
| Any swipe off a flick key | Flick key | Never starts a handwriting stroke or a hint flick | None |
| Swipe off a letter flick key that turns, reaches another letter where no arm points, or travels far | Flick key, glide typing on | Glides a word; anything shorter and straighter types the arm, overshoot included | Flick reach (141%), A flick onto a letter glides a word (Off) |

While the finger is down the cross popup shows every arm with the chosen one lit (Flick popup, On), and the key's face draws its arms small at their edges and corners (Show flick arms on keys, On).

## Dragging from a modifier key

Every layout has a <KeyCap>Shift</KeyCap> key. A <KeyCap>Ctrl</KeyCap>, <KeyCap>Alt</KeyCap> or <KeyCap>Meta</KeyCap> key only exists on a layout you built or imported, since none of the shipped ones carry a modifier row. Add one from the [layout editor](/languages/custom-layouts/#editing-a-key). Full mechanics on [Gestures](/typing/gestures/#dragging-from-a-modifier-key).

| Gesture | Where | What it does | Setting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tap | <KeyCap>Shift</KeyCap> or a modifier key | Arms it for the next key you press | None |
| Double-tap | <KeyCap>Shift</KeyCap> or a modifier key | Caps lock, or locks the modifier until you tap it again | None |
| Drag onto a letter and lift | <KeyCap>Shift</KeyCap> | Types the capital, through the composing buffer | None |
| Drag onto anything else and lift | <KeyCap>Shift</KeyCap> | Sends Shift plus that key (Shift+Enter, Shift+Tab) | None |
| Drag onto another key and lift | Ctrl, Alt or Meta | Sends that combination, then clears the modifier | None |
| Drag off and back, then lift | Either | Arms it, the same as a tap | None |
| Drag off the key grid and lift | Either | Cancels, and nothing is sent | None |

## Dragging from ?123 or ABC

The layer shows for as long as you hold, and the keyboard is back on the layer you started from the moment you lift. Full mechanics on [Gestures](/typing/gestures/#dragging-from-123-or-abc).

| Gesture | Where | What it does | Setting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Drag onto a key and lift | <KeyCap>?123</KeyCap> | Shows the symbols, types the one you lift on | None |
| Drag onto a key and lift | <KeyCap>ABC</KeyCap> on a symbol or custom layer | Shows the letters, types the one you lift on | None |
| Drag off the first symbols page | <KeyCap>?123</KeyCap> while symbols are up | Shows the second symbols page instead | None |
| Hold still over a key mid-drag | Any key with alternates | Opens its popup, which your finger then steers | Ignores Choose without lifting |
| Drag onto a layer, mode or tool key and lift | Any of them | Nothing is typed, and the layer goes back | None |
| Drag off the key grid and lift | Anywhere outside | Cancels, and nothing is typed | None |
| Tap without moving | <KeyCap>?123</KeyCap> or <KeyCap>ABC</KeyCap> | Switches layer and stays there, as always | None |
| Hold still, tap a key with another finger | <KeyCap>?123</KeyCap> | Runs that key's chord instead of typing it; <KeyCap>L</KeyCap> switches language out of the box | Chords with ?123 (On); each chord bindable. See [Gestures](/typing/gestures/#chords-with-123) |

## Using the volume keys as a cursor

<SettingsPath path="Typing / Volume keys" />

Off by default, and only active while the keyboard is on screen. As soon as it closes, the volume keys go back to controlling volume. See [Gestures](/typing/gestures/#volume-keys-as-a-cursor-control) for the swallow-press-and-release detail.

| Gesture | Where | What it does | Setting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Press volume down | Hardware volume rocker | Moves the cursor one character left | Volume keys move the cursor (Off) |
| Press volume up | Hardware volume rocker | Moves the cursor one character right | (same) |
| Hold a volume key | Hardware volume rocker | Repeats via Android's own hardware key-repeat | (same) |
| Press while audio is actually playing | Hardware volume rocker | Falls back to real volume control instead of the cursor | Release the keys while audio plays (On) |

## Swiping with several fingers

<SettingsPath path="Typing / Gestures / Multi-finger swipes" />

| Gesture | Where | What it does | Setting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Two fingers swiped up together | Anywhere on the letters | Selects all | Multi-finger swipes (On); each slot bindable |
| Two fingers left / right | Anywhere on the letters | Undo / Redo | (same) |
| Two fingers down | Anywhere on the letters | Paste | (same) |
| Three or four fingers, any direction | Anywhere on the letters | Nothing until you bind it | (same) |
| Fingers that head different ways, lean past 35°, or one that lifts first | Anywhere on the letters | Types as usual | None |

Every finger has to travel about 28&nbsp;dp the same way before the swipe is recognised. Two-finger swipes are off while two-thumb typing is on.

## Pinch and stretch

<SettingsPath path="Typing / Gestures / Pinch and stretch" />

| Gesture | Where | What it does | Setting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Two fingers spread apart | Anywhere on the letters | Splits the keyboard (nothing if already split) | Pinch and stretch (On); Stretch bindable |
| Two fingers pinched together | Anywhere on the letters | Merges the keyboard (nothing if not split) | Pinch and stretch (On); Pinch bindable |
| A second finger that lands late, a still finger beside a moving one, a pair that drifts, or a finger that lifts first | Anywhere on the letters | Types or glides as usual | None |

Both fingers land within about 0.3&nbsp;s, the gap changes by 40&nbsp;dp and a quarter, and each finger moves 14&nbsp;dp along the line between them. The action runs at the lift. Off on the floating keyboard and while two-thumb typing is on.

## Holding a greyed-out button

A greyed-out button, chip or switch can't be pressed yet, but it can still be held. Hold it for the press and hold delay and it says why, in the same small bubble that names an icon button when you hold it.

| Gesture | Where | What it does | Setting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hold a greyed-out AI action | [AI panel](/tools/ai/) | Says there is no text to act on yet | None |
| Hold greyed-out **Replace**, **Insert** or swap | [Translate panel](/tools/translate/) | Says nothing is translated yet, or that the text is already in that language | None |
| Hold greyed-out previous or next | [Media controls](/tools/media/) | Says the app that is playing doesn't allow it | None |
| Hold greyed-out undo | Voice bar | Says there is no phrase to take back yet | None |
| Hold the greyed-out check mark | Spelling bar of the [word card](/smart/personal-dictionary/) | Says the spelling has to change first | None |
| Hold greyed-out **Tidy** or **Combine** | [Clipboard panel](/tools/clipboard/), with clips picked | Says how many text clips it needs | None |
| Hold a greyed-out setting | Any settings screen | Says why, with **Open the setting** when another setting unblocks it. See [Asking why a setting is greyed out](/reference/settings/#asking-why-a-setting-is-greyed-out) | None |

The bubble stays up for about four seconds. When the reason names a setting, tap the bubble to open the settings app at that setting. Holding a greyed-out button never presses it.

## Details & edge cases

- **The two spacebar slots resolve by hold time, not distance.** A fast flick and a careful drag can cover the same ground, so the keyboard can't tell them apart by how far you've moved, only by whether the press-and-hold delay has elapsed yet.
- **Numpad is a discrete action.** Unlike Cursor or Language, which keep responding for as long as you drag, a slot set to Numpad only needs the initial slop crossed once; everything after that is inert.
- **Two settings hide the keyboard on a downward swipe, two words apart.** "Swipe down to hide the keyboard" under Typing → Gestures applies to the spacebar. A separate "Swipe down to hide" under Appearance → Toolbar applies to the toolbar strip. Turning one on doesn't touch the other, and both ship off.
- **The 2-D cursor touchpad only claims the vertical axis once a drag has actually resolved to Cursor.** With the defaults (quick swipe = Language, press-and-hold swipe = Cursor), a fast downward flick within the press-and-hold delay is still governed by the quick-swipe slot and dismisses the keyboard rather than scrolling through lines.
- **<KeyCap>⌦</KeyCap> is the same gesture pointed the other way, and shares every setting but its own on/off switch.** Swipe deletes, Show what you will delete and the two step distances govern both keys. No built-in typing layout carries a forward delete key; add one in the [layout editor](/languages/custom-layouts/), where it is also an action the [text-editing pad](/tools/text-editing/) can carry.
- **A delete key on a panel is a delete key.** The text-editing pad's <KeyCap>⌫</KeyCap> and <KeyCap>⌦</KeyCap> run the swipe, the preview and the word-clearing hold, and repeat at the delete interval rather than the pad's own. Before, they only tapped and repeated.
- **Backspace's word-delete acceleration resets on a reversal, not on release.** Dragging right mid-swipe doesn't bring back a deleted word. It only means the next leftward word costs full travel (72&nbsp;dp) again instead of the shrunk amount.
- **Volume-key cursor control re-checks "is audio playing" on every key press**, not once at the start, so Release the keys while audio plays hands control back and forth live as playback starts and stops mid-typing.
- **The press-and-hold delay has one slider.** Key press → Timing runs 100–800&nbsp;ms, the whole range stores. Accessibility → Touch no longer has its own copy: it links straight to Key press → Timing instead.
- **The backspace repeat has its own wait, not the press-and-hold delay.** Holding <KeyCap>⌫</KeyCap> starts repeating after Time before a key repeats (300&nbsp;ms), which is a separate slider. Lengthening the press-and-hold delay for accent popups no longer slows backspace down with it.
- **A very wide screen can let the floating panel's resize grip briefly exceed its 500&nbsp;dp maximum mid-drag.** It snaps back the moment you release.
- **With TalkBack or another screen reader running, most drags above stop working unless you turn on gesture pass-through.** Set **TalkBack support** to **Gestures** under Accessibility → Screen reader. Then grant the "WM Keyboard gestures" accessibility service. Those two steps keep the spacebar cursor slide, the backspace word-swipe, glide typing, and handwriting alive underneath TalkBack's own touch exploration. Without that service granted, all four fall back to Explore-by-touch and don't respond to a drag. The Play Store build has no Gestures mode, so there these drags stay off under a screen reader. See [Accessibility overview](/accessibility/overview/#the-gesture-pass-through-service).
