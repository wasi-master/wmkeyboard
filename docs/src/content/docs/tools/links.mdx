The **Link wallet** keeps links you open often as buttons in the keyboard. Tap a button and the link opens in your browser; hold it to type the link into the field, copy it, share it, rename it, change it, move it or delete it. There is no sync and no account behind it. The links stay on your phone, in the keyboard's own storage, and travel to another phone only inside a [backup](/privacy/backup/).

## Opening it

Tap **Link wallet** on the toolbar, or in the toolbox if it isn't pinned (see [the toolbar](/tools/overview/)). Tap it again to close it. Typing `bookmarks` into a text field offers the tool as a chip on the suggestion strip.

The wallet reads its file from the phone's protected storage, so it can't open before you unlock the phone for the first time after a restart.

## Adding a link

1. Copy the link in your browser or wherever you found it.
2. Open the wallet and tap **Add** in the header.
3. The address box opens with the copied link already in it, and the key rows come up underneath. If what you copied was a whole message, the first link in it is picked out. Type or fix the address, or tap **Paste** to put the copied text in again.
4. Press <KeyCap>Enter</KeyCap> or **Next**. The box now holds the name for the button, starting with the site's name, such as `github.com`. Change it or leave it.
5. Press <KeyCap>Enter</KeyCap> or **Save**. The link joins the end of the list and the keys go back to your app.

An address without `https://` in front, like `example.com/page`, gets it added. Links that open an app, such as `mailto:` or `geo:`, are kept as they are. Anything that isn't an address is refused with a note under the box, and so are `javascript:` and `file:` links, which a tap must never run.

**Cancel** under the box drops the link without saving it.

## Using the buttons

Each link is a button with the first letter of its name in a disc, the name, and the site under it, two or more to a row depending on your screen's width. Tap a button to open the link. If no app on the phone can open it, a short message says so.

Hold a button to pick it up. It turns to the selected colour and a row of actions comes up along the bottom of the panel:

- **Type it** types the link into the field behind the keyboard.
- **Copy** puts it on the clipboard.
- **Share** opens Android's share sheet with the link.
- **Rename** opens the name box with the button's name in it. <KeyCap>Enter</KeyCap> keeps the new name.
- **Change link** opens the address box with the link in it. <KeyCap>Enter</KeyCap> moves on to the name, which keeps what it was.
- **Move back** and **Move forward** move the button one place at a time. The first button has no Move back and the last has no Move forward.
- **Delete** takes the link out, with an Undo bar for a few seconds.
- **Cancel**, or holding the same button again, puts the row away.

With a hardware keyboard, the arrow keys walk the buttons and <KeyCap>Enter</KeyCap> opens the one with the ring.

## Backups

The links, their names and their order are a section of their own in a backup, **Link wallet**, on by default. Restoring a backup replaces the links on the phone with the ones in the file; the wallet picks them up the next time it opens.
