The first test build of 0.6.8. Autocorrect reads each hand's taps as a shape,
glide can start on the number row and pause on a key for its flick letters, and
Thumb-Key and MessagEase grids arrive for 43 languages. Voice gains offline
Qwen3-ASR and models straight from Hugging Face. Hundreds of settings now carry
a help picture behind their "?", and there is a mouse-mode trackpad, a privacy
shade, a Link wallet and a weather forecast in the calendar.

### Typing and autocorrect

- Taps are read as a shape, with a drift shared across the word and each
  hand's own spread. Autocorrect also works in web address fields.
- Capitals in every field, Capitals by app to overrule an app's own, a double
  space that can be Undo, and double-space timing down to 50 ms.
- Your own pairs for Close brackets for me, and typing rules that rewrite what
  was just typed.
- Stacked dead keys, curly apostrophes and old Hangul syllables.
- The space no longer capitalizes words mid-sentence, and a fix never drops a
  word's prefix.

### Glide

- A swipe can start on the number row; pause on a key mid-glide to pick one of
  its flick letters.
- Run a stroke into delete to drop a word, or lift it on a suggestion to take
  it. A stroke across the spacebar offers its runner-up phrase.
- German compounds read with their linking s and n; Japanese kana swipes lean
  on shape, and a Kana JIS stroke can voice a kana by crossing shift.
- An option to show next words after a glide, and a sentence weight setting.

### Suggestions and dictionaries

- First-word predictions for 71 more languages, subtitle word pairs for 34,
  and a context model for Hinglish.
- Arabic words complete behind their prefix and offer their pronoun endings;
  Portuguese verbs take a hyphened pronoun.
- Definitions in 19 languages' own words, dict.cc list import, regex search in
  word lists, and a word's shortcut finds it.
- The offensive-word lists for English and fourteen more languages stop hiding
  everyday words.

### Layouts and languages

- Thumb-Key and MessagEase grids for 43 languages, with Thumb-Key's
  out-and-back actions.
- Nepali and Sanskrit phonetic layouts, Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu and Kannada
  the Varnam way, Telugu KaChaTaThaPa, and jquery.ime schemes with rule-file
  import.
- The Indic Keyboard boards that had no match here, Northern, Inari and Skolt
  Sami, and Chewing for whole-phrase Zhuyin.
- Convert script, and Sanskrit in another script, on vidyut-lipi.
- One Import a layout row with a sheet of its sources. Keys go down to 24 dp,
  with separate column and row spacing.
- Switch a language off without deleting its downloads, and give each
  language its own suggestion text size.

### Voice and AI

- Offline Qwen3-ASR, and speech models straight from Hugging Face. Choose the
  engines the voice tool offers.
- Chat answers read aloud, with a microphone to ask and cut in.
- Presets for OpenAI-compatible services, eight new local models, and Brave's
  research mode. Deep research runs on a model on the phone.
- MCP servers sign in with OAuth and can reach local programs through a
  bridge.

### Tools

- Trackpad mouse mode: a pointer that clicks, scrolls and drags in the app.
- Privacy shade as a black screen or your own photo, a Link wallet of saved
  links, and a dashboard of each tool's permissions, last use and space.
- Calendar: swipe between Month, Day and Agenda, with the next 24 hours and
  the week's weather.
- Clipboard: Clips in rows, expiry after 5 to 45 minutes, collect copies into
  one clip, share several pictures at once.
- Notes open an Obsidian or Zettel Notes vault beside the built-in notes.
- Built-in search that needs nothing set up, and sums with units through
  Numbat.
- Shake the phone to run any keyboard action. Custom buttons can open any app
  screen, through Shizuku or root too.

### Themes and settings

- Variable font axes and font motion, thirty Material shapes for keys and
  popups, Letter outline over photos, and a glow that can light the
  neighbouring keys.
- Help pictures for hundreds of settings behind their "?".
- Export to a file is encrypted under the backup passphrase.
- Release notes open by themselves after an update.
