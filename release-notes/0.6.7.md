Autocorrect now reads the taps of a whole phrase, across missed spaces, in
English, German, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese. Plugins move to API 5
with a Pythonic `wm` package, permissions you grant per plugin, and panels drawn
like the keyboard's own. A second toolbar row, honeycomb layouts, a see-through
split gap, a Files tool, a privacy shade and 36 legacy Indic fonts arrive too,
and typing is lighter on slower phones.

### Typing and autocorrect

- Autocorrect reads the taps of a word whole, across missed spaces, and reads
  the word before again once the next one lands. It learns how widely each
  hand's taps scatter. Whole phrases are read in German, Spanish, French,
  Italian and Portuguese as well as English.
- "ie" and "eg" get their stops, and an emoji picked from the panel corrects
  the word before it.
- A switch for the underline on corrected words.
- The first Backspace after a paste removes the whole paste.
- **Delete everything** before or after the cursor, or the whole field in one
  tap, and **Type the clipboard** for fields that refuse a paste.
- Line swipes on delete go a drawn line at a time through a wrapped paragraph,
  each can be turned off on its own, and ⌦'s swipe shows what it will delete.
- Shift mid-word cycles the strip's chips through lower, Title and UPPER.
- Next-word suggestions get a switch and a count, the phrase chip offers your
  own phrases, and a predicted word stays on the strip while you type it.
- Import your own `.wmlm` language model, with a strength slider, and a
  word-pair pack for Hinglish ([#1292](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1292)).
- Web and email addresses count as one word. Blacklist entries can be regex
  patterns.
- Glide: a stroke for a word no list has offers the letters it marks
  ([#1291](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1291)), German,
  English, Finnish, Estonian and Hungarian get measured profiles, and a taught
  correction applies on the next swipe.

### Languages and layouts

- Honeycomb layouts: Hex QWERTY and Hex ABC.
- Ergo-L, AdNW, KOY, Rulemak, Bribri and SwiftKey's older Arabic board, and ظ
  gets its own key on the Arabic board.
- Import CLDR keyboard3 (LDML) layouts, FlorisBoard's new format.
- Legacy font output for 36 newspaper and DTP fonts, among them Kruti Dev,
  Walkman Chanakya, Bamini, TSCII, Nudi, AnmolLipi, Preeti, ML-TT, FM and
  Sanskrit 99 ([#1252](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1252)).
- An iOS-style Japanese flick pad with ABC and number pages, which takes
  repeated taps too. Gboard readings become conversion candidates, Mozc
  included. Same sound searches homophones of the character before the caret.
- Arabic is spell-checked by its rules with Ayaspell.
- Any enabled layout can be the secondary board, and there is a one-handed
  layout.
- Bottom row presets, and a hold list you build on Enter, comma or 🌐.
- Pinch and stretch on the keys merge and split the keyboard. The split gap can
  be see-through, so the app behind it takes taps
  ([#1256](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1256)).
- Inverse top corners curve the app into the keyboard
  ([#1325](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1325)), and
  full-screen typing works with the phone upright.
- Spacebar swipes can run any action. A Quick punctuation preset puts , ? ! on
  the period key.
- On a hardware keyboard, Alt+Space and Shift+Space can change the language,
  and letter keys can follow the on-screen layout.
- The long-press hint can sit in any corner of a key, and key holds can follow
  Android's delay.

### Toolbar and tools

- A second toolbar row of tools you pick, arranged by dragging
  ([#1217](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1217)).
- Button sizes over the theme, a resize handle, and apps as tool buttons
  ([#1264](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1264)). Swipe up
  anywhere on the strip to open the tools.
- Custom buttons for app shortcuts, setting switches and settings pages
  ([#1213](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1213),
  [#1236](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1236)).
- A tool left open in an app comes back when you return to it within three
  minutes, a time you can change.
- More tools float, and any tool pops out into its floating box
  ([#1265](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1265)). The floating
  button gains phone controls: ringer, volume and radios
  ([#1326](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1326)).
- A Files tool with two lists and your own folders
  ([#1267](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1267)).
- A privacy shade over the app above the keyboard, which can come up by itself
  in apps you choose.
- A document scanner in the keyboard; scans can go to your own Paperless-ngx
  server, and the Gallery can browse and upload to your Immich server
  ([#1205](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1205)).
- Calendar: a Day view where events are dragged to a new time, weather on
  events ([#1212](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1212)), and
  publishing an event to Mobilizon or Pretix
  ([#1274](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1274)).
- An on-device notes assistant for headings, summaries and reminders
  ([#1293](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1293)).
- Clipboard: a Text only switch, code and Markdown clips drawn as such, email
  addresses with Compose email, a shared board on a WebDAV folder, and tracking
  parameters removed from copied links. Copies go through one write gate, and
  hidden secrets stay in memory only
  ([#1215](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1215)).
- Translate reads a translation aloud, puts your languages first and
  translates only when asked.
- Timestamp types the date and time in one tap. Mwmbl is a web search
  provider, Almaany joins the Dictionary's Open in menu, and a Memes chip comes
  from KLIPY.
- Search shortcuts such as `@gif cats`, and the contact chip can open
  WhatsApp, Telegram or Signal.
- The app launcher takes icons from icon packs installed on the phone
  ([#1275](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1275)).

### Plugins

- Plugin API 5: a Pythonic `wm` package, permissions in tiers you grant from a
  dialog, and calendar, to-do, clock, notifications, location, files, sensors
  and scanning on the phone.
- Plugins can be full tools with their own icons, float in the box, open from
  `@keyword` chips or a key, keep secret settings, ship translations, and reach
  your own server on the LAN.
- Python gains classes, decorators, `with` and a standard library. The plugin
  editor completes and checks the `wm` package, and the web editor runs every
  API 5 call.
- Plugin panels look like the keyboard's own, fill the panel, and reopen on
  the tab you left.

### Voice and AI

- On-device AI on the NPU of Dimensity, Tensor and Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 phones.
- Import any whisper.cpp ggml model, or pick one trained for a single language.
- Mask swear words, Suppress symbols, a sound of your own when the mic opens,
  and a Smart Turn that only waits longer.
- Whistle on the F-Droid build, as an opt-in download.
- Tool calls that weak models write wrong are read anyway, and a call that
  never ran is never reported as done.

### Themes

- Per-key gradients, glow and radius, and placed orbs.
- FUTO key pictures, icons and wallpaper colours import, and each theme can
  carry a FlorisBoard-style rule sheet.
- Import a font from the theme editor. Key fonts draw lining figures, so serif
  digits sit on the line.
- Power saving can wear a dark theme and turn suggestions off, and a mode can
  follow dark mode or change what Enter does.

### Settings and backup

- Undo settings changes and save restore points
  ([#1242](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1242)).
- Search from any page, recent pages, and a step's siblings in the breadcrumbs
  ([#1317](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1317),
  [#1318](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1318)).
- Switched-off settings show greyed out with the reason.
- Backups get a schedule and contents per location.
- Snippets: edit them all as one text document, picture snippets, `{ask:}`
  fill-in fields, and `{greeting}`, `{battery}`, `{list:N}` and more.
- Export the personal dictionary to a text file.
- A feedback form, opened by a shake or a screenshot, and a Debug info screen
  for bug reports.

### Speed and fixes

- Typing is lighter on slower phones: the key grid is drawn as one layer, the
  keyboard adapts to the phone's load, and background work that slowed typing
  on a Helio G99 is gone. Low-memory TV boxes get a lighter keyboard.
- Android 7 no longer crashes on time zones.
- A fingerprint sensor's key no longer ends the word being typed.
- Android's Paste, Copy and Cut keys use the private clip.
- Effects stay inside the board, a photo fades into the board instead of the
  app, and key lighting stays on the key face.
- A full clip never ends dictation, and live voice engines keep the mic.
- Many smaller fixes in glide, the strip, the clipboard, the clock, snippets,
  stickers and the layout editor.
