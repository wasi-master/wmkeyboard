Plugins now run Python instead of Lua. One-row boards come in 36 languages,
Avro included, and a smart punctuation key reads the sentence and offers its
mark in 58 languages. Keys can take Expressive Keyboard's materials and Keys
Cafe's press splashes, Parakeet can run on the Snapdragon NPU, and WM Keyboard
can be the phone's digital assistant and its voice input.

### Typing and autocorrect

- Hold a word on the suggestion strip and pick **Correct to…** to teach
  autocorrect what it should become: type `kagay`, correct it to `lagay`, and
  the next `kagay` is fixed for you.
- Number-row slips are fixed more readily when you type fast: two digits in a
  word ("y97" becomes "you") and a digit typed as a word's first letter
  ("5yped" becomes "typed"). Numbers such as "5pm", "4th" and "10am" stay as
  typed.
- **Capitalize every word**, beside Automatic capital letters.
- A smart punctuation key that reads the sentence and shows the mark it
  expects, in 58 languages, with a Standard and a Large model and a switch per
  language.
- Choose which marks the automatic space follows.
- A switch hides the underline under the word being typed.
- Undo and redo backspace from the strip, and a Retype button on the strip or
  the recent words' row ([#1189](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1189)).
  Two-thumb words get their own Backspace choice ([#1188](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1188)).
- A long word borrows room from short ones on the strip, and next-word
  predictions fill the extra slots and the pages ([#1151](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1151), [#1154](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1154)).
- Suggest characters by their Unicode name.
- Swipe up or down on delete to delete whole lines ([#1162](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1162)).
- Two-letter words get their accents back, and the last number-row and
  space-bar slips from [#1107](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1107) are fixed.
- Glide: each language can have its own error radii ([#1136](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1136)), a word fixed
  the same way three times teaches the stroke, and learned shapes move to a
  checked binary file ([#1130](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1130)).
- Pick several words to delete together from the personal dictionary
  ([#1134](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1134)).

### Languages and layouts

- One-row boards in 36 languages, Avro among them, and a One-row QWERTY with
  the alphabet on a single row. They are a short strip in Minuum's manner:
  one key tall, the letters bare on the board at one size, each set a little
  higher or lower by its row on a full keyboard.
- Unexpected Keyboard's QWERTY grid as an English layout.
- Samsung's Indic boards show vowel signs after a consonant, with Samsung's
  page order. Gboard and Samsung layouts carry their own symbol pages.
- Let it choose the word on eight more Indic languages.
- Avro takes up a Bengali word under the caret with its suggestions, and a
  key over a selection spells a word. The word card's spelling bar types Avro
  Bangla and takes a paste ([#1190](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1190)).
- Romanized dictation brings Banglish and Hinglish back in the spelling a
  reader expects.
- Fancy Text gets 18 more styles, zalgo at the level you pick, and a strip you
  arrange.
- Flick labels are sized and placed per key. A key in place of 🌐 can be any
  tool.
- Ctrl+Shift alone can change the language on a hardware keyboard ([#1128](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1128)).
- A removed language stops suggesting and takes its downloads with it.

### Themes and icons

- Expressive Keyboard's looks: Soft, Pixel and Dot-matrix materials, Orbs and
  Meld effects, and Pop, Squish and Fade press motions.
- Keys Cafe style press splashes, key colours and key motions, and several
  keys styled at once from the editor's preview.
- **Your icons**, a library of imported pictures for any icon or key.
- The theme gallery opens at once and scrolls without stutter.
- A faint incognito mark behind the keys.

### Plugins

- Plugins are written in Python, run by a small interpreter built in Rust, with
  editor tooling and a plugin editor that speaks Python. Lua plugins are
  retired.
- Plugins can add selection actions, and Clink actions run.
- Step budgets are three times larger, so heavier plugins such as Math Mode
  finish.

### Voice and AI

- WM Keyboard can be the phone's digital assistant and its voice input.
- Parakeet and Moonshine on the Snapdragon NPU, opt-in GPU builds of Parakeet,
  and Parakeet shows the words while you speak and ends a phrase at the pause.
- Vosk as an offline dictation engine.
- GGUF models on the Snapdragon NPU, and Privatemode AI as a built-in,
  encrypted provider.
- A recognizer that cannot start hands the phrase to the next one, and spoken
  punctuation survives a recognizer's own marks.

### Tools

- Open tools in a floating box over other apps, with Translate inside it.
- The floating button gains a thin pill shape, actions per swipe, Back, recents
  and quick settings, and up to sixteen quick actions.
- Apps on the toolbar, and a launcher with floating, split and pair modes.
- A Resources screen shows memory, CPU and loaded models.
- Clipboard: photo and video metadata (location, camera, dates) is removed
  before pasting ([#1179](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1179)), filter chips combine with a hold ([#1187](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1187)), and Paste next
  on a held clip.
- Dictionary: recorded pronunciations from Wiktionary and Forvo, a looked-up
  word read in its own language, and Takoboto, Shinjikai, Jisho, Wiktionary
  or your own site in the Open in menu ([#1150](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1150), [#1161](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1161)).
- Translate: "… in spanish" translates in place or opens Translate ([#1139](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1139)), and
  Firefox translation files come from a mirror when Mozilla's server fails.
- Clock alarms stay in reach after their notification is swiped, and can move
  to the phone's clock app ([#1144](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1144)). The task-app sync stops copying tasks over
  and over ([#1164](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1164)).
- Stickers: AI keyword suggestions, and Change title and keywords from a
  sticker's hold sheet ([#1157](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1157)).
- Sums work in number fields, and AllRatesToday is an optional currency rate
  source ([#1019](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1019)).

### Settings and backup

- Pick several layouts, icon packs or sticker packs and export them in one file.
- Hold symbols take several per box, get Gboard and Samsung presets, and set
  the number row's digits.
- An encrypted backup opens on every version, Play's included, and a page on
  the docs site converts new backups for 0.6.3.
- Long settings screens open without one long frame.

### Fixes

- The keyboard no longer leaks memory each time it restarts, which could run a
  phone out of memory after a long day of app switching.
- Back with the keyboard up closes only the keyboard.
- The floating keyboard no longer lets the system's keyboard bar cover the
  app's text field.
- One-time code logs list only notifications that could have held a code, and
  the settings say how to lift Android's code redaction.
- Each file picker opens on its own folder ([#1131](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1131)), and a new-settings walk
  step opens like a search result ([#1111](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1111)). The theme editor says when Reduce
  motion holds moving looks still ([#1129](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1129)).
- LiteRT loads on phones that ship their own copy, and on-device AI names a
  missing library instead of saying wait.
- Many smaller fixes in the clock, the clipboard, glide, settings links and the
  layout gallery.
