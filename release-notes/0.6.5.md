The keyboard now works with WM Pass: it types logins and codes from the
password manager, keeps a password book on its vault, and fills or saves key
fields without holding a secret of its own. Modes can follow the app, the time
of day, the screen's direction or a hardware keyboard. Romanized typing covers
77 more languages, 341 more get word lists, and every Gboard and Samsung
Keyboard layout this one lacked is here. Chinese layouts float words over the
keys, a theme can be copied from a screenshot, and Notes, To-do, Clock,
Calendar and Send files have new panels.

### WM Pass, autofill and privacy

- A WM Pass tool and code chip type from the password manager. The keyboard
  asks for one value when tapped and keeps nothing; each side checks the
  other's signing certificate first.
- The tool is a password book on WM Pass's vault: search every kind of item,
  type or copy each field, add a login, or change a password with WM Pass's
  generator ([#1077](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1077)). WM Pass's buttons can sit on the strip.
- Key fields fill from and save to WM Pass, and a backup location can be
  picked from WM Pass or given to it.
- A Password manager button shows in password fields that have nothing to
  fill ([#1003](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1003)).
- A secret wiped off the clipboard leaves the history too. A copy made in a
  password field can stay in history, and with incognito's clipboard pause
  off, private copies join it ([#1026](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1026)).
- Sync with the copy of the keyboard in a private space or work profile, over
  a paired, encrypted local link ([#1017](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1017)).
- One-time codes are found in Chinese, Japanese and Korean messages, with
  your own code words ([#992](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/992)), in message lists and in the shade, and the
  keyboard says when Android hid a code from it ([#1049](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1049)).

### Languages and typing

- Romanized typing for 77 more languages, on rules of our own: 56 through the
  Indic engine, 17 Arabic-script, and Yiddish, Mongolian, Santali and Manipuri.
- Word lists and word pairs for 341 more languages, from HPLT, MADLAD-400,
  Sangraha, Wikipedia and Tatoeba text. 80 languages get their first list.
- Borno phonetic for Bangla, on Codepotro's published keymap. Bangla starts
  with no space after a picked suggestion ([#986](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/986)), joins common endings onto a
  known stem, and ranks the downloaded list by real word counts.
- Mistyped romanized spellings are corrected like English typos.
- Greeklish and Wylie phonetic layouts, four Sorani layouts ([#972](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/972)), Godan and
  50-key Japanese, and AZERTY with the community layout's holds.
- Contracted braille through liblouis.
- Selection actions add furigana to Japanese, tashkeel to Arabic and niqqud to
  Hebrew, switch script, restore ё and fix half-spaces. Turkish is checked by
  its own rules with hunspell-tr.
- Choose the language each app opens on ([#1040](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1040)).
- The Dakshina dataset is merged into eleven romanized lists, and 1,700 more
  real spellings join the Bangla known-spellings list.

### Layouts and keys

- Gboard's and Samsung Keyboard's layouts: 650 Gboard boards and 1,773
  Samsung ones that had no match here, 316 and 286 new languages with them,
  and their press-and-hold letters added to the layouts that already matched.
  Samsung's Moakey, Vega, Cheonjiin Plus and 12-key Chinese and Zhuyin pads,
  handwriting pads and Korean 10 key.
- A key can show only upright or only sideways, and a layout can draw another
  layout when sideways.
- One press can run several text editing actions ([#985](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/985)). Swipe up on
  backspace to clear the whole box; Commit every key as a mode setting
  ([#1024](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1024)).
- The punctuation keys can keep what was last picked from them ([#1096](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1096)). Close
  quotes as well as brackets.
- The spacebar has a hold delay of its own ([#1060](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1060)), and a setting says how far
  a space swipe has to go ([#1088](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1088)).
- A key's hint can move on its own, and the hint size no longer moves it.
  Caps lock looks different from Shift on.
- Edit the further pages of a paginated alphabet. Built-in layouts come right
  after your own ([#1038](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1038)).
- The currency on the d and ?123 keys follows your language and region.
- Keyman layouts are fetched instead of shipped.

### Modes

- A mode can follow a kind of app (any browser, terminal, messenger or mail
  app), the screen's direction, a hardware keyboard or a time of day ([#1025](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1025)).
- Keep a mode out of named apps and rank modes by priority; bind one to the
  Enter key's action.
- Per mode: incognito, key sound, vibration, a line-break Enter, one-handed
  mode and snippet triggers.

### Suggestions, autocorrect and glide

- Two letters typed the wrong way round are put back ([#1105](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1105)), and a space hit
  for a letter or a letter hit for a space is fixed ([#1107](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1107)).
- The autocorrect confidence slider reaches 1.0, with two-edit fixes below 1.5
  ([#1055](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1055)). A rare spelling is asked about at once, and only once ([#1047](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1047)).
- Your own words show before you type, and right-to-left scripts can keep a
  left-to-right strip. A word no list knows yet can wear its own colour
  ([#1098](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1098)).
- Edit learned fixes, slips and undos one by one.
- Typing spells unlisted words the context model could reorder, and glide reads
  them too, with a blend of its own for German ([#1016](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1016), [#1051](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1051)).
- A stroke across the spacebar reads as one phrase, with a tick at each
  crossing, one-letter words in the chain, and Backspace walking back through
  it.
- A close stroke decodes wider before the context model reads it.
- A Canadian, British or Australian list keeps American spellings off the strip
  ([#999](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/999)). An apostrophe inside a word doesn't end it on delete ([#1032](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1032)).
- Chips can sit at the far end of the strip, the join chip with them ([#1066](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1066)).
  The date chip takes your own formats ([#1089](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1089)).

### Chinese, Japanese and Korean

- Words float over the keys on conversion layouts: English words you added,
  Han words over the key their reading continues on, fuzzy-spelling matches,
  and Jianpin, Double Pinyin, T9 and next-word ([#1028](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1028)).
- The conversion decoder and its tables run in Rust, with packs mapped.
- Wubi, Yale, Cantonese Pinyin and ClearFlow Pinyin.
- The conversion strip offers English words you taught it and learns its picks
  ([#1033](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1033)). A reading that spells a trigger or keyword offers a chip ([#1029](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1029)).
- Pinyin learns the letters a reading went in as ([#1101](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1101)), and a tap inside the
  reading edits it ([#1106](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1106)).
- Korean gets Hanja on the strip and Fix spacing through Kiwi.
- RIME's Taiwan and Hong Kong forms apply under every schema ([#1083](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1083)).

### Tools

- A file manager over the keys for tools that pick a file: Gallery, Send files,
  the AI chat, KDE Connect and the scanners stop bouncing out to Android's
  picker. Not on Play, which does not allow the permission.
- An Import page in Settings ([#983](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/983)), and settings search finds the buttons
  that import things.
- Notes: a shelf of cards or a timeline ([#560](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/560)), Save to notes from any app's
  share sheet ([#997](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/997)), Obsidian export, and search that lists matching passages
  ([#1099](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1099)), also from the clipboard search ([#1104](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1104)).
- To-do: task cards, a due carousel, quick due dates, reminders, repeating
  tasks and a strip chip when one falls due ([#920](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/920), [#995](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/995)).
- Clock: seven tabs, icons for alarm, timer and stopwatch, a count per alarm,
  and snooze until answered. Calendar: month, agenda and composer.
- Send files: a tray of destinations, a radar for nearby devices, and LocalSend
  and PairDrop devices in Nearby.
- Symbols: swipe between categories, edit them, browse all of Unicode, search
  it by name, and hold a symbol for its relatives.
- Dictionary: Urban Dictionary as a source (off until turned on), 31 languages'
  words with English meanings offline ([#1085](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1085)), StarDict imports, and an entry
  that follows the search bar ([#1036](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1036)).
- AI: ask while a word is typed ([#1048](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1048)), and list a provider's models.
- Add any tool with a panel to the home screen, each with its own icon ([#970](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/970)).
- Handwriting: pen thickness and colour, fading ink and a taller panel. The
  magnifier stays above the keyboard.
- Speed test: the dial is the button and the result is a ticket.
- Split screen through Android's own switch, with an opt-in helper.
- Translate changes languages from the keys with /ja or /bn:ja, and has a clear
  button ([#1092](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1092)).

### Themes, emoji and stickers

- Copy a theme from a screenshot of a keyboard, down to each key's shape.
- Pick colours from the wallpaper's Material You shades.
- Make your own Fancy Text fonts from a pasted alphabet.
- Emoji the phone can't draw are hidden by default ([#1044](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1044)). Search the Mixes

- Move or copy stickers to another pack ([#1061](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1061)). Import LeanType sound packs;
  key sounds can keep to headphones or the speaker ([#1021](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1021)).

### Clipboard, backup and offline

- Clipboard tag filters get their own row, can leave a tag out, and let go on
  close ([#1084](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1084), [#1093](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1093)). A held clip's buttons follow your order ([#1094](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1094)).
- Backups to Box, pCloud, Synology, QNAP, NFS, MEGA, iCloud and Proton. A
  backup comes back whatever its size ([#1070](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1070), [#1072](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1072)). RIME's words travel
  with it, and downloads can be saved to a zip ([#1045](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1045)).
- The offline build imports the context model, romanized dictation's model,
  FUTO Swipe's model ([#998](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/998), [#1000](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1000), [#1002](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1002)), the Mozc dictionary ([#1006](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1006)) and
  Firefox translation models ([#1035](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1035)).
- Data export for automation, behind its own switch ([#1076](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/1076)).
