Downloadable word-frequency lists, offensive word lists and emoji keyword
dictionaries — sources and licences
==========================================================================

The wordlists the app can download (Settings > Languages > Dictionaries)
are packaged in the WM Keyboard data repository
(https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard-data), which redistributes data
from the open-source projects below. Some datasets have been reformatted,
trimmed, compressed or reorganized; original attribution and licensing
remain unchanged. The repository's data/README.md carries the complete
per-language attribution table; this file lists every source and its
licence.

Word-frequency lists
--------------------

  hermitdave/FrequencyWords (OpenSubtitles 2018)
  https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords — CC BY-SA 4.0
  (most languages)

  Leipzig Corpora Collection
  https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/ — CC BY 4.0
  (about a hundred languages)

  Wikimedia data dumps
  https://dumps.wikimedia.org/ — CC BY-SA 4.0
  (about two hundred smaller-Wikipedia languages)

  rspeer/wordfreq
  https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq — MIT
  (bn, ca, hi, ja, ta, uk)

  Per-language sources: urduhack/urdu-words (ur),
  kasunw22/sinhala-para-dict (si),
  vigneshwaran-chandrasekaran/tamil-language-words-list (ta),
  tahmid02016/bangla-wordlist (bn), the Official Lojban Dictionary (jbo),
  the Klingon Dictionary at klingonska.org (tlh), the Ardalambion Quenya
  wordlist (qya), the lipu Linku Toki Pona dictionary (tok),
  motaitalic/devanagari-documentation (bho, brx, doi, ks, mai, raj),
  Balochi datasets by Zaanthai and mainkilora (bgn), Unicode CLDR (blo),
  Chakma datasets by amlan107 and dipongkar01 (ccp), the eBible Corpus and
  Glot500 (quc), the eBible Corpus with arndri/rohingya-tweet-id and
  freococo/rohingya_asr_audio (rhg), the ETCBC Peshitta Syriac Corpus
  (syr), and the Cantonese Wikipedia dump with OpenCC (yue). Each keeps
  the licence stated in the data repository's attribution table (CC BY-SA
  4.0 / CC BY-SA 3.0, MIT, Apache-2.0, or public domain).

  Dakshina dataset (Google Research)
  https://github.com/google-research-datasets/dakshina — CC BY-SA 4.0
  (merged into the romanized lists: bn, gu, hi, kn, ml, mr, pa, si, ta, te,
  ur)

  Romanized lists: Malayalam via knadh/ml2en over the FrequencyWords list;
  Roman Urdu from Khubaib01/RomanUrdu-NLP-Sentiment-Corpus (Apache-2.0).

Chinese word pairs (the Pinyin "Chinese word pairs" pack)
---------------------------------------------------------

  Word and word-pair counts, not text: OpenSubtitles 2018 Simplified
  Chinese via OPUS (https://opus.nlpl.eu/, Lison and Tiedemann 2016) — the
  same subtitles as the FrequencyWords zh_cn list above — and the Leipzig
  Corpora Collection's zho-cn_web_2015 and zho_news_2020 sentences
  (CC BY 4.0), split into words with the CC-CEDICT pinyin pack.

--------------------------
