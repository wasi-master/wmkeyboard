Offline Parakeet downloads NVIDIA's parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3 speech recognition
model (https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3), as exported for
ExecuTorch with 8-bit activations and 4-bit weights by an ExecuTorch
maintainer (https://huggingface.co/larryliu0820/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3-executorch).
The model is not bundled with the app; it is downloaded on request.

Changes: quantized and exported for ExecuTorch by the publisher above. The
keyboard runs it unmodified.

Offline Parakeet can also download Parakeet Ultra by Moondream
(https://huggingface.co/moondream/parakeet-ultra), a post-trained
parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3 under the same licence, as exported for ExecuTorch by
WM Keyboard (https://huggingface.co/wasi-master/parakeet-ultra-executorch).

Changes: converted to NeMo's layout, quantized to 8-bit activations and 4-bit
weights, and exported for ExecuTorch, by
https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/tree/main/tools/parakeet-ultra.
Ultra's voice-activity head is left out.
